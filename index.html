Why this topic matters

HIV in Kyrgyzstan is not an abstract issue. It is a real public health problem, and it is also a science communication problem. When people do not get clear and honest information, fear fills the gap. According to official Kyrgyz data (2025), over 15,000 people are registered with HIV.
